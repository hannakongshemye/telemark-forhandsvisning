Become a stockist

You can sell our jars in your shop, or use the sauerkraut and kimchi in the food you make in your catering business or restaurant. Get in touch and we will sort out the rest together.

For your shop

The jars suit the shelves of farm shops, health food shops and grocers. They are already sold at Life helsekost on Tuven in Notodden and in many farm shops around Telemark. All seven are organic and Debio certified, come in a small and a large jar, and are a chilled product kept at 0 to 6 °C.

For your catering or restaurant

The sauerkraut and kimchi go straight from the jar into the food you make. Three catering companies in Telemark already use three of our products in a lefse roll where every ingredient is from Telemark. We use the kimchi in sushi, on burgers and in salads, and the sauerkraut with pork and sausages. If you need larger amounts, ask us.
