Sauerkraut with caraway

Cabbage and caraway with a little crushed juniper

Traditional Norwegian sauerkraut, though it does not taste like the one from the shop. Just cabbage, caraway and a little crushed juniper, with no sugar or vinegar.

How we use it

With pork ribs, pork and sausages, and a classic at Christmas. If you find it too sour, drizzle a little honey over it.

Recipe: Hot rye toastie with sauerkraut and Jarlsberg

Recipe: Medister patties with sauerkraut and potatoes

Good to know

Organic and Debio certified. Fermented in a stoneware crock for fourteen days, with no added sugar or vinegar, and not heat treated. A chilled product, keep at 0 to 6 °C. Read about storage

Tip: it is good with a little fat alongside, such as melted butter, olive oil, sour cream or mayonnaise. It brings out the flavour.
