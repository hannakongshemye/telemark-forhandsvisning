Red cabbage with spices

With a hint of clove, allspice and cumin

Fermented red cabbage with a hint of ground clove, allspice and cumin.

Organic red cabbage is hard to find locally, so we are working with growers nearby to grow it for us from the 2027 season.

How we use it

With Christmas food and game.

Recipe: Christmas tacos with pork belly and red cabbage

Good to know

Organic and Debio certified. Fermented in a stoneware crock for fourteen days, with no added sugar or vinegar, and not heat treated. A chilled product, keep at 0 to 6 °C. Read about storage

Tip: it is good with a little fat alongside, such as melted butter, olive oil, sour cream or mayonnaise. It brings out the flavour.

Ingredients and nutrition

Ingredients and nutrition will be added when we have the new labels.

Buy it

You will find the jars at the REKO rings in Bø, Skien, Porsgrunn, Notodden and Kongsberg, at Life helsekost on Tuven and in farm shops around Telemark.
