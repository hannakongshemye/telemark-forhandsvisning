Where to buy

These are the places you will find the jars.

REKO rings

REKO is a Facebook group where you buy straight from the producer. Join the group for your ring, order in the comments under our post, and collect your jars at the hand-over. There is nothing to pay in advance and no delivery charge.

We sell at these five rings:

  • Midt-Telemark (Bø)
  • Skien
  • Porsgrunn
  • Notodden
  • Kongsberg

Shops

Life helsekost on Tuven in Notodden was the first shop to sell the jars. Today you will also find them in many farm shops around Telemark.
